Revaro Community Rules

By joining Revaro, you agree to the following:

  • Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or targeted attacks on other users.
  • No inappropriate content. Nudity, explicit content, and illegal material aren't allowed in events, comments, or profile images.
  • No spam or fake events. Don't post events that don't exist or pretend to be an organizer.
  • Event accuracy. Events can change or get cancelled. Always check the organizer's official page before you go.
  • Your responsibility. You attend events at your own risk. Revaro is a discovery platform and isn't affiliated with the events listed.

Breaking these rules can get your content removed or your account suspended.
